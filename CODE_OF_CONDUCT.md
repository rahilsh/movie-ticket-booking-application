# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from feedback.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing others' personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct, trolling, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `rahilrshk@gmail.com`. The maintainer will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may edit or remove contributions or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
